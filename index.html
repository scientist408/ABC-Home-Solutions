PaRaJai Home Solutions LLC is a women-owned business founded by professionals with backgrounds in microbiology, education, and household management. This unique combination of expertise allows us to approach home care with the precision of scientific disciplines, the organization of experienced educators, and the practical understanding of everyday household needs.
